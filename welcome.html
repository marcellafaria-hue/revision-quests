📚 Welcome to Revision Quests!

A collection of fun, extra revision games for Grade 7 English

Hi everyone! This page is home to all the revision games we've made together this year. Each one is a quick, optional activity you can do on your own, whenever you like, no pressure, no grades, just a fun way to practice what we've been learning in class.

🗂️ What you'll find here

  • Grammar games — conditionals & wish, verb patterns, possessives & reflexive pronouns, in case & unless
  • Literature games — The Tempest, Hunt for the Wilderpeople, Address Unknown
  • Language review games — classroom English, numbers, countries, and more
  • Women writers & pen names — Jane Austen, Virginia Woolf, and the authors who hid behind other names
  • A special end-of-year reflection activity for the end of Grade 7

🎮 How it works

Pick any game, play at your own pace, and see your score at the end (where there is one). Some games are solo quizzes, one is a team game for the whole class to play together, and one is just for quiet reflection — no score at all.
