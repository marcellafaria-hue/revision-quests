Twenty-Four Doors

A Christmas English calendar for Grade 7 · open any door, think together, share with the class

How to use it
  • Students sit in small groups. Open doors in any order, or press "Surprise me".
  • Each door has a timer. Groups talk, write their ideas on a mini whiteboard or paper, then share with the whole class.
  • Change speakers each time so everyone gets a turn.
  • "Reveal" shows answers or ideas. Opened doors stay open; click one to see it again.
