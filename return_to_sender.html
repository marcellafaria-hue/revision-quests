Grade 7 · Term 3 revision · Address Unknown · Women Writers · Grammar

How it works
  • Open any envelope. Topics run across the top; levels go from 1 (warm-up) to 4 (hardest).
  • Students work in small groups. Each group talks it through and writes its answer on a mini whiteboard or paper.
  • Quick pick and Fix it: groups show their answers at the same time, then explain their choices before you reveal the answer.
  • Huddle: groups discuss, then a speaker from each group shares with the whole class. Use "What a strong answer includes" to sum up together.
  • Change speakers every time so everyone gets a turn to talk to the class.
