The Fireside Tales
Christmas Folklore Curiosities

Test week is over, so here's a cozy, optional activity just for you. Sit by the fire, read four short texts about surprising Christmas legends from around the world, and answer a few questions about them. No pressure, just good stories.

📖 How it works

  • There are 4 short texts. After each one, you answer 4 questions. Then there is a final round with questions about all four texts.
  • You can look back at the text at any time while you answer. Reading carefully is the point!
  • You'll see instantly if you're right, with a short explanation.
  • Each text has a small word help box for new vocabulary.
