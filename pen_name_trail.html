The Pen Name Trail

A solo weekend quest about the women writers who changed English literature: secret pen names, Jane Austen, Virginia Woolf, and the tricks writers use to make their words unforgettable.

📜 Traveler's Notebook — a few names to know

  • Pen name / nom de plume: a fictitious name an author writes under.
  • Aphra Behn: often called the first woman in England to earn her living as a professional writer.
  • Jane Austen: wrote Pride and Prejudice (1813).
  • Virginia Woolf: wrote A Room of One's Own (1929); said a woman writer needs money and a room of her own.
  • Hypophora: when a writer asks a question and answers it right away.
