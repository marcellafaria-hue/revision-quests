A Christmas Carol
The Three Spirits Quiz

Can you remember what happens to Ebenezer Scrooge on one very strange Christmas Eve? A cozy, optional quiz for the holidays, based on Charles Dickens's famous story (1843).

📚 Step 1: Before you play, read it or watch it!

  • 📖 Read it. A Christmas Carol is a short book (a novella), and it is free online: Project Gutenberg. You can also read it in a school library or in an adapted version.
  • 🎬 Or watch a film adaptation. For example: Scrooge (1951), The Muppet Christmas Carol (1992) or Disney's A Christmas Carol (2009). Ask someone at home which one to choose.

Films change some small details. The questions follow the main story, but if your film is different from a question, the book is the reference.

🕯️ Who's who?

  • Ebenezer Scrooge: a rich, mean old businessman
  • Jacob Marley: Scrooge's old business partner, who is dead
  • Bob Cratchit: Scrooge's poor clerk (office worker)
  • Tiny Tim: Bob Cratchit's youngest son
  • Fred: Scrooge's cheerful nephew
